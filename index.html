Crop insurance and farmers' income

College research, 2025 to 2026
  • Surveyed 161 farmers in Erode district with a structured questionnaire and interviews.
  • Analysed the data in SPSS using percentage analysis, one-way ANOVA, independent t-tests and chi-square tests.
  • Farmers rated "financial security during crop loss" highest (mean 4.30 out of 5), but 60.9% reported claim delays at least sometimes; crop-cutting experiments and administrative processing were the main causes.
  • Annual income was significantly related to the claim amount received (χ² = 33.77, p = 0.001), and occupation to how the claim was used (p = 0.018).
  • Recommended faster claim settlement, simpler bank procedures and local awareness programmes.

SPSS, survey design, hypothesis testing
